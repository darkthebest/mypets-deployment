My Pets - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Summary

My Pets is a pet-care guide with a feeding timer. It does not collect, store, upload, or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party trackers. It does not require an internet connection.

Local-only data

The feeding timer history is saved only on your device using standard local storage. It never leaves your phone. Deleting the app removes this data permanently.

Notifications

The app can show feeding reminders using your device's notification system. Turning this on is optional and can be disabled at any time in your device settings. Notification scheduling happens entirely on your device.

Children

This app is intended for general audiences and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Contact

For any privacy questions, contact the developer via the store listing you downloaded this app from.
