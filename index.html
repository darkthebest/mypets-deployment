Feeding guide

Pick the animal, then read the stage that matches the babies' age. Amounts are per bottle-feeding — always feed by weight, not appetite.

Formulas & recipes

Commercial milk replacer is always best. Homemade formulas are for emergencies only — until you can get real kitten/puppy milk replacer. Never feed plain cow's milk.

Feeding timer

Orphaned babies need round-the-clock feedings. Set the interval for your baby's stage and the app will remind you — day and night.

Emergency & warning signs

Neonatal babies go downhill fast. If a baby feels cold, is not eating, or is not gaining weight, act immediately.

My Pets is an informational guide, not a substitute for a veterinarian. When in doubt, call your vet or nearest rescue as soon as possible — even at 2am.
